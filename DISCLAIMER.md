Although the software is developed with care, it has not undergone formal certification, independent security auditing, or comprehensive validation for business-critical or regulated environments.

Incorrect settings, software defects, interrupted operations, or unexpected system conditions could potentially result in incomplete backups or unintended data loss.

To minimize these risks, we recommend:

- Verifying backup configurations before relying on them.
